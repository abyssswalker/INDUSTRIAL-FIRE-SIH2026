WHAT IS GEOCONTEXT-AI?

A geospatial intelligence platform engineered to identify anomalous and potentially critical industrial fire activity by integrating real-time satellite thermal observations, high-precision metric clustering, and spatial proximity models.

Industrial Spatial Context

Multi-Signal Classification
